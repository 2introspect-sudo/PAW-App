# People Are Wild — Companion App (Prototype)

An interactive companion to *People Are Wild: A Field Guide to Understanding Human Behavior* by Stacy McCoy, LCPC (Root & Crown Books).

The app is a single, self-contained web page (`index.html`). It runs in any modern browser on phone or desktop, with no build step and no server.

## What's inside

- **Mapping the Wild**: the book's five landmarks and their zones, each linked to its chapters
- **Five encounters**, one per landmark, each structured around the PAW method (Pause, Assess, Witness) and ending with Field Guide Prompts
- **The Ensemble**: five interconnected characters and a relationship map
- **Book Owner Access**: expanded chapter content (key ideas, chapter PAW steps, Field Guide Insights, prompts)
- **Notes from the Field**: private reflections, stored only in the reader's browser
- **Internal Campsite**: grounding tools from Chapters 6, 10 and 11

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser.

To publish it online with GitHub Pages: **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Deploy from a branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then save. The app will be live at `https://2introspect-sudo.github.io/PAW-App/`.

## Prototype notes

- Characters, their backstories and the four new encounter stories are placeholder drafts.
- Chapter 2 is placed at Trigger Canyon and Chapter 6 at Inner Compass Clearing as draft mappings, pending confirmation in the manuscript.
- The book-owner check is a demo (the first word of Chapter 8's Closing Note).
- Character art is cropped from the concept mockup and is not final.
